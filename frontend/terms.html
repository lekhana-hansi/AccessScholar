Terms of use

AccessScholar helps people discover education funding information. Listings are provided for informational purposes and do not constitute an award, admission decision, or guarantee of eligibility.

Verify before applying

Always review current eligibility, deadlines, funding, and application instructions on the scholarship provider's official website. Providers control applications and make all decisions. AccessScholar does not represent or imply endorsement by listed organizations.

Eligibility matching

Profile matches compare only available, structured published criteria and may not interpret all conditions. A “Strong Match” is not a guarantee or provider decision. Confirm every requirement directly with the provider.

Reports

Reports are submitted for review and do not automatically change or remove a listing. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a report.

External services

Links to official portals and external sources are provided for convenience. Their content, availability, and terms are controlled by their respective operators.
