Privacy policy

AccessScholar does not require an account to browse public scholarship listings.

Information stored in your browser

Accessibility preferences and an optional student profile are stored in your browser's local storage when you use those features. Profile data is not sent to the scholarship API unless you choose “Find Scholarships For Me”; that request is used to compare your profile with published criteria and is not saved by the matching endpoint. You can clear the saved profile from the profile form or clear this site's browser storage.

Scholarship reports

If you report a listing, the scholarship ID, selected issue, and details you provide are sent to the AccessScholar server for admin review. Do not include personal, medical, or other sensitive information. A report is not treated as a verified correction.

Third-party websites

Scholarship and source links lead to external provider websites with their own privacy practices. Review those policies before submitting an application.

Contact

For application questions, contact the provider through its official portal. AccessScholar does not process scholarship applications.
