Accessibility statement

AccessScholar is designed to support keyboard use, visible focus, semantic page structure, responsive layouts, form labels, and screen-reader announcements. The floating accessibility button provides text sizing, readable font choices, display modes, reading aids, focus highlighting, and browser speech controls.

Keyboard use

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to activate buttons, and Escape to close an open dialog. Use the “Skip to main content” link to bypass the main navigation.

Media and browser support

The website currently contains no video. Any video added in the future should include captions. Speech and voice selection depend on browser and operating-system support. Fonts such as Open Sans and Atkinson Hyperlegible are used when available on your device; otherwise the selected fallback font is used.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility controls are assistive preferences and do not replace operating-system or browser accessibility features. A contrast checker estimate does not audit every page element. No formal WCAG conformance claim is made; real assistive technology and audit testing are still required.

Browser extension

The optional Chrome extension is separate from the website and must be installed and enabled by the user. A website cannot turn a browser extension on automatically.
